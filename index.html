Scholarship eligibility







Needs marks ≥ 80, attendance ≥ 85, income ≤ 50,000. Marks ≥ 90 earns the full scholarship.


Pharmacy sale check







A sale larger than stock is rejected. Remaining stock below 10 triggers a restock warning.
